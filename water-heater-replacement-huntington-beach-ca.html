Warning Signs

Warning Signs Water Heater Replacement Services Should be Used

Your Water Heater is Getting Old

Many tank heaters have a useful lifespan of approximately 8-12 years depending on water quality or other conditions. Unless one has a particularly high volume of use, heaters typically only require replacement after 8-12 years of normal use.

You See Water Around the Tank

Finding water around the tank can result from a leaking pipe, a faulty pressure-releasing valve, or even an overall leaky tank. If the tank itself is faulty or leaking, these issues should be addressed by replacing the heater. A water heater leaking in Huntington Beach should be inspected to identify the source before deciding which solution is needed.

The Temperature of the Hot Water Frequently Changes

If the hot water in a customer's home becomes too hot and then too cold, their heater might require replacement services. A technician can inspect the tank and any other components to determine if an adjustment, repair or full system replacement is necessary.

Repairs Are Becoming Regular and Cumbersome

A major cause for heater replacement is an ongoing series of repairs. After the costs of multiple repairs are factored in with the age of the system, a customer might find it more cost-effective to opt for full system replacement services.
