When Should I Install/Replace My Garbage Disposal?

If you are looking to install a new disposal, or upgrade your kitchen, here are some reasons why you may need to install a garbage disposal.

Replacing Your Old Garbage Disposal

Not always will you need to replace your disposal, however there are signs you may need to.

Common signs include:

  • The disposal regularly jams
  • There is constant leaking from the body, mount or pipework.
  • Unusual grinding noises from the disposal
  • The disposal keeps cutting out.
  • The disposal is struggling to process waste.
  • The disposal is showing signs of damage, such as rusting, cracking etc.

These are just general signs and may not be caused by the disposal. Leaking could be caused by the drain connection. Jamming could be caused by something getting caught in the grinding chamber. A professional can check if it needs repairing or replacing.

Choosing The Right Garbage Disposal

The best garbage disposal will depend on how much cooking your household does, the layout of your kitchen, what features you want and how much space you have to install it.

Continuous Feed Or Batch Feed

There are two common types of garbage disposal.

Continuous feed disposal units will run whilst switched on. This means you can slowly feed suitable food waste into the disposal. This is commonly found in residential kitchens and can be a great option for regular meal prep.

Batch feed disposal units will run once you have placed a compatible lid or stopper in place.

This will depend on your personal preferences, the installation requirements and how you use your kitchen. Be sure to check the manufacturers guidelines on how to use the unit and what safety features it has.

Motor Power, Noise And Kitchen Requirements

There are a variety of garbage disposals available with different motor powers, grinding chamber, noise reduction features and size. Consider how much suitable food waste your household produces.

Consider:

  • Motor power: Choose a unit that will fit the manufacturers recommended usage and your household needs.
  • Noise reduction: Some units will have insulation and design to reduce the noise. However, no unit should be completely silent.
  • Size: Ensure the unit will fit underneath your sink and not cause any issues with storing, piping or other equipment.
  • Mount: Ensure the sink and mount will fit with the chosen model.
  • Warranty: Check what warranty the manufacturer offer in writing. There may be conditions for installation.
  • Usage: Ensure you know what food waste can go into the disposal and how to look after it.

Choosing a suitable model will ensure it will perform well, fit, be easy to use and will work with your current kitchen.

How Will My Garbage Disposal Be Installed?

Depending on whether you are installing a new unit, replacing an old unit, changing your sink or modifying your pipework and electrical connections.

Sink, Drain & Space

Your technician will first check your sink drain, mount, cabinet space and drain. Ensure your sink is compatible with your chosen disposal and there is enough space in your cabinets.

Check the drain setup. If you are using a dishwasher connection, ensure the disposal will fit with your chosen model and if any modifications are needed.

If you have older pipework, damaged fittings or an unusual sink layout, extra work may be required.

Electrical & Dishwasher Connection

You will need the correct electrical connection. Ensure the switch will work with the disposal. Depending on your current set up and chosen model, you may need to plug into an electrical socket, utilise an existing socket or have fixed wiring.

Ensure you follow the manufacturers guidelines and any requirements. If your current circuit, socket, switch or wiring isn't suitable you may need a qualified electrician to make the changes. Never attempt electrical modifications unless you have the knowledge and precautions.

If you are using a dishwasher connection, ensure it is set up correctly. Some disposals will have a dishwasher inlet that will need to be prepared. Follow the manufacturers guidelines to ensure you don't have any issues with the connection.

Mount & Test

Once you've confirmed the disposal is compatible, your technician will fit the mount and attach the disposal as per the manufacturers guidelines. Connect the sink drain and discharge piping.

Check for any leaks around the flange, mount, discharge and dishwasher drain (if applicable). Check the electrical connections are working.

Finally check everything is working as it should. Your technician will explain how to use your chosen model.

Replacing Or Repairing

Not always will you need to replace your disposal. If your disposal is jammed, the reset button has tripped, or there is a loose connection, it may be possible to repair.

Consider:

  • Age: If your disposal is very old and showing signs of wear and rust it may be more suitable to replace. If it's fairly new and has a small issue it may be possible to repair.
  • Issue: If something is blocking or there is an issue with the drain connection it may be possible to remove. However if there is an issue with the motor it may not be.
  • Leak: If there is a leak from the pipework it may be possible to repair. However if there is damage to the disposal it may be more practical to replace.
  • Parts: If there are suitable parts available and the mount is in a good condition it may be possible to repair.
  • Kitchen Upgrade: If you are upgrading your kitchen or the way your household uses it, it may be a good idea to choose a different model.

Never put your hand in the disposal, even if it looks like it's turned off. Always follow the manufacturers guidelines and turn off the power. If your disposal is jammed, leaking near the electrical components or keeps cutting out, stop using it and seek the correct help.

A professional can help determine if it's an issue with the appliance or pipework/electrics.

Common Installation Issues

Incorrect installation can cause leaks, drainage issues, excessive noise and the appliance not working properly. Here are some common issues to be aware of.

  • Poor Sealing: Leaks can occur around the flange and drain fittings. Ensure everything is aligned and sealed correctly.
  • Incorrect Discharge Piping: This can cause unnecessary stress on the connections. Ensure the piping fits with the discharge from the disposal.
  • Not Enough Cabinet Space: This can cause issues when trying to install. Measure how much space you have.
  • Incorrect Electrical Connections: This can cause issues with how it works. Ensure you follow the manufacturers guidelines and any electrical requirements. A qualified electrician may be needed.
  • Incorrect Dishwasher Inlet: This can cause issues with how your dishwasher drains. Ensure you know if your chosen disposal needs any preparation.

Don't force anything together that isn't compatible. Not all disposals will have the same mounting. Follow the manufacturers guidelines for your chosen model.

FAQ

How Long Will It Take To Install My Garbage Disposal?

The time it takes to install your garbage disposal will depend on your sink set up, the model and whether you are replacing an existing unit. A simple replacement may not take as long as if modifications are needed. Ask your professional for an estimate.

Can I Install A Garbage Disposal In Any Kitchen Sink?

No. You will need to ensure it will fit in the sink opening, mount, cabinets, drain and electrical. Some sinks may require extra parts or modifications. Check the manufacturers guidelines before purchasing.

Do I Need A Plumber To Install My Garbage Disposal?

It can be beneficial to have a professional plumber install your garbage disposal. This includes mounting to your sink, connecting to the drain, discharge piping and dishwasher. If there are any electrical changes needed an appropriately qualified electrician may be needed.

If i need a plumber to install a disposal, I should confirm that the provider offers this specific service and can handle the required plumbing connections. Anyone searching for a licensed plumber near me should verify the provider's licensing or credentials where applicable.

Can I Replace My Existing Garbage Disposal With A Different Brand?

Often yes. However you shouldn't assume it will fit. Different models may have different mounting, size, electrical and connection requirements. Check the specifications of your new unit.

Why Is My Garbage Disposal Leaking?

This can be caused by a lack of sealing around the flange, loose or incorrect drain connection, issues with the dishwasher hose connection or the disposal itself. Identify where the water is coming from. If it is near any electrical components stop using the appliance.

What Should I Not Put Into My Garbage Disposal?

Always follow the manufacturers guidelines. Grease, oil, fibrous food waste and hard materials can cause issues. Many disposals will not be able to handle these items. A garbage disposal shouldn't be used as a substitute for a bin.

Should I Repair Or Replace My Old Garbage Disposal?

This will depend on the issue, condition of the unit, parts available and cost. If it's a small jam or drain connection it may be possible to repair. If it's heavily corroded, damaged housing or internal failure it may be best to replace. A professional can help identify the practical options.
