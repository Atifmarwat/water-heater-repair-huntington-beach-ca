Common Problems With A Garbage Disposal

Knowing the issues your garbage disposal may have will help you to give the right description. A local plumber near me can inspect the disposal and help identify the cause of the problem.

Humming But Not Grinding

If your disposal is humming but not grinding then it may be due to a blockage. This can happen due to food scraps, fibrous vegetables, bones and anything else that shouldn't go in there. It can also be due to a faulty or worn part.

Switch your disposal off immediately if it is humming. Leaving it to run can cause the motor to overheat. Don't put your hand in there either. A professional can tell you if it can be cleared or if it needs fixing.

Garbage Disposal Not Turning On

It may be due to an electrical issue. This could be due to it tripping, power issues, faulty switch or faulty motor.

If you have noticed your disposal isn't working at all then don't constantly turn it on and off. Find out why it's not working. A professional can check the electricity and parts of the disposal to find out what the likely issue is. If i need a plumber for an electrical or mechanical disposal problem, I should stop using the unit and arrange a professional inspection.

Leaking From Underneath

This may be due to loose drain connections, faulty gaskets, worn seals or cracks in the disposal. If it is due to a faulty seal inside or damage to the body then simply tightening won't help.

Stop using your disposal if you have noticed water pooling in the cabinet. Water can cause damage to the materials in there and can cause an electrical risk. Contact us to arrange an inspection to find out where the leak is coming from. If the problem needs urgent attention, searching for an emergency plumber near me may help you find a suitable professional.

Drainage Issues

Even though you have a garbage disposal you will still have drainage issues. This may be due to food build up, greasy deposits and general debris. You may also have rattling or grinding noises due to utensils or hard items.

If your kitchen sink is running slowly it may be due to a blockage in your disposal, blocked drain connection or blockage further down. If it is making grinding noises once switched off then this should also be looked into. For related drain problems, plumber services near me may include inspection of the disposal and connected pipework.

Common Reasons For Issues

Knowing what could be causing the issue will help prevent it from happening again.

  • Foreign Objects: Utensils, bottle tops and other hard items can cause blockages.
  • Fibrous Food Scraps: Celery strings, corn husks etc. can cause wrapping around moving parts.
  • Grease & Cooking Oils: Can cause build up in drain pipes.
  • Over Loading: Too much food waste can cause issues.
  • Worn Parts: Seals, connections, switches and moving parts can cause issues.
  • Electrical Issues: Power, switch, wiring or motor can cause it not to work.
  • Poor Installation: Poor drain connections can cause leaks, vibrations and issues.

These are possible reasons for your issue. There can be many reasons for the same issue so it's best to check that the disposal and pipework are in good condition.

Garbage Disposal Repairs

Depending on whether the issue is with the disposal, electrical connections or pipework, a professional can determine which repairs are needed. If you are looking for garbage disposal repair Huntington Beach, describe the symptoms and ask what inspection and repair work may be required.

Unblocking Blockage

If the issue is a blockage then the first thing to do is turn off the power and identify the blockage. Some disposals have a manual override to free the blockage that came from the manufacturer. Others may require a different fix.

A technician can check for any trapped items and see if the grinding parts can be returned to normal. If it is still blocking then it's best to sort out the cause.

Resetting & Electrical Issues

Some disposals have a reset button. If the motor is overloaded then this can trip. However this isn't a full fix.

Repairs may be needed to the power, switch, connections and motor. Electrical issues should be dealt with safely. Turn the power off and open up the disposal only according to the manufacturer's instructions and if it is safe to do so. If there is damaged wiring or a faulty motor then more repairs may be needed.

Fixing Leak

This can be due to a loose connection between the disposal and sink, dishwasher inlet or discharge pipe. This can be fixed by tightening the connection, replacing with a suitable gasket.

If it looks like the disposal body is leaking then it is probably easier to replace. Make sure you know where the leak is coming from.

Smelly Disposal & Drainage Issues

If you have a constant smell then this can be due to food build up inside, build up around the splash guard or build up in the drain. It can also be due to a blockage causing slow drainage.

Cleaning the easily accessible parts according to the manufacturers instructions may help with general build up. However if you are having constant issues with smells or slow drainage then it may be best to look into the disposal and pipework. Don't use harsh chemicals in the drain unless specified by the manufacturer. This can cause damage or safety issues.

Should I Repair Or Replace?

This will depend on how old your disposal is, what the issue is, if parts can be found and how much it will cost to repair.

You may want to repair if:

  • There is a blockage that can be removed or connection that can be repaired.
  • A suitable part can be found.
  • The motor and body are in good condition.
  • The cost to repair is reasonable.

You may want to replace if:

  • The disposal body has cracks or is leaking from somewhere that can't be repaired.
  • The motor is faulty and it wouldn't be cost effective to repair.
  • It keeps having issues despite having repairs.
  • Replacement parts are hard to find or it is heavily worn.

A professional may need to check the mounting, sink hole, drain connection, electrical connections and dishwasher connection. A new disposal will need to be compatible with the current set up and followed according to the manufacturers instructions.

If it keeps breaking down then compare the cost of repairing it with the cost of a suitable replacement. Ask what is included. Before choosing a licensed plumber near me, verify the provider's licensing status where applicable and confirm that they can perform the required work.

How To Avoid Issues In Future

Having good habits with your disposal will help you avoid unnecessary blockages, smells and drainage issues. Always follow the manufacturers instructions for your specific model.

Top Tips:

  • Use cold water whilst running and shortly after if recommended.
  • Feed food scraps in slowly.
  • Don't put utensils, bottle tops or hard items in there.
  • Avoid putting in greasy items, cooking oils or large amounts of fibrous food.
  • Clean easily accessible splash guards and other areas according to the manufacturers instructions.
  • Be aware of any changes to the grinding noises, drainage and vibrations.
  • Check underneath your cabinet regularly for any dampness.

Don't put your hand in there to remove any debris. Turn the disposal off and disconnect the power according to the manufacturers instructions. If there is an item stuck in there, it keeps blocking or there are electrical issues then contact a professional.

FAQ

When Should I Get My Garbage Disposal Repaired In Huntington Beach?

If your disposal is constantly blocking, not turning on, leaking from underneath, making constant strange noises or having drainage issues then you should contact a professional. If you notice any leaking, burning smells or electrical concerns then stop using it. For huntington garbage disposal repair, explain the symptoms and ask the provider to assess the problem before recommending repairs.

Why Is My Garbage Disposal Humming?

A humming disposal usually means it is getting power but the grinding isn't turning. This can be due to a blockage or mechanical failure. Turn it off straight away to avoid overheating. Get the cause looked into.

Can I Get My Garbage Disposal Repaired?

This will depend on the issue. If it is blocked, some leaking connections or there are some replaceable parts then it can be repaired. If it has cracks in the body, faulty motor or is constantly having issues then it may be easier to replace.

Why Is My Garbage Disposal Leaking From Underneath?

This can be due to a leaking connection from the sink mounting, gasket, dishwasher inlet, discharge pipe or from the disposal body. Knowing where it is coming from will help. Stop using it until you know where it is coming from. Especially if it is near any electrical components.

Why Is My Garbage Disposal Constantly Tripping?

Constant tripping can be due to constant blockage, motor overload, electrical issues or faulty parts. Don't treat the reset as a permanent fix. If it keeps happening then stop using it and get it inspected.

Is A Blocked Garbage Disposal The Same As A Blocked Kitchen Sink Drain?

No. A blocked garbage disposal could be due to a blockage in the disposal meaning the food waste isn't being processed. A blocked drain can cause issues with the water running through. It may be due to both.

How Much Will Repairs Cost In Huntington Beach, CA?

This will depend on the diagnosis, labour, parts, accessibility and if there is any extra plumbing/electrical work. Ask for a quote for your specific issue. If it is heavily worn or damaged compare it to replacement.

What Should I Do Before My Repair?

Stop using the broken disposal. Clear the area underneath the sink. Note down any issues. If there is any leaking or electrical concerns then don't use it. If you can find out what brand/model it is then have this to hand.
