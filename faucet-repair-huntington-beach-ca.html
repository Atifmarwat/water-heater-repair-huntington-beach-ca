Common Issues With Faucets

There are several moving parts and connections to water in your faucet that can become worn over time. Knowing what the issue is can help us provide the correct repair. If you are looking for faucet repair Huntington Beach, describing the symptoms can help a professional identify the likely cause.

Leaking/Dripping

If your faucet is still dripping after turning off the handle then there could be a worn cartridge, damaged washer, damaged seal or a problem with the valve. This depends on your faucet design.

You could also experience leaks around the handle, base of the faucet, connections or underneath your sink. This should be different to any leaks coming from the spout.

Even a small leak should be attended to. Water dripping around the base of your faucet or in your cabinet can cause damage. If the leak is coming from a supply line or connection under your sink then turning off the relevant shutoff valve may help prevent any water damage. If the leak needs urgent attention, searching for an emergency plumber near me may help you find a professional. Confirm the provider's availability before arranging a visit.

Low Water Pressure/Uneven Flow

If the water coming from your faucet is not flowing as it normally would then there could be a blockage to your aerator, limescale build up, a partially closed shutoff valve or a blockage in your supply.

The aerator is a small attachment on the end of many spouts. This helps shape the water flow and can cause build up of limescale or debris. Sometimes cleaning or replacing with a compatible replacement aerator can help.

If several faucets are suffering from low pressure then this could be an issue with your home's water supply. A professional can help identify what the issue is. Searching for plumber services near me may help you find a provider who can assess the faucet and water supply.

Loose Handle

If your handle is becoming loose, sticking or is difficult to turn then there could be a loose fixing, worn parts or damaged handle. Continued force can cause more issues.

Some handles can be tightened or repaired however some will need replacement parts specific to your model. If your handle is struggling to control the water then the valve inside could need checking.

Noisy Faucet/Water Temperature Issues

If your faucet is making whistling, rattling or strange noises then there could be a blockage, loose parts or another issue. If it is only making a noise when a particular fixture is being used then this can help identify the cause.

If you are having issues with controlling the hot and cold water then there could be an issue with the cartridge, mixing valve or water supply. Single handle faucets can suffer from a worn cartridge causing issues with the flow and temperature. Other designs may have separate valves or parts.

If the water is suddenly becoming too hot or cold then this could be an issue with your water heater or overall water supply.

Reasons For Issues With Your Faucet

There are many reasons why your faucet may be having issues. Knowing what the issue could be can help us provide the correct repair.

  • Worn Parts: Cartridges, washers, O rings and seals can become worn.
  • Limescale Build Up: Limescale can build up around your aerator and inside your faucet.
  • Loose Connections: Movement, installation issues or worn parts can cause leaks under your sink.
  • Corrosion/Damaged Parts: Older faucets can have parts that are difficult to remove or not working correctly.
  • Over Tightening: Over tightening can cause issues with parts inside.
  • Water Supply: Low pressure, partially closed valves or blockages can cause issues.

There could be several reasons why your faucet is having issues. For example one could be a worn cartridge causing the spout to drip and another could be a damaged valve seat. A professional should be able to identify what the likely cause is.

Cartridge/Washer Replacement

Many modern faucets use cartridges to help control the water flow and temperature. Some faucets use compression valves, washers or other parts.

If there are worn parts inside your faucet then replacing the correct part can stop your faucet from dripping or improve the handle. You may need to dismantle your faucet after turning off the water supply safely. Our technician can then check the relevant parts and see if a replacement is suitable.

It is important to use a compatible part. Not all cartridges and seals will be compatible.

Fixing Leak Issues

Leaking from the base or under your sink could be due to issues with your supply hoses, threading, fixings or seals. The issue should be addressed to stop the water.

Over tightening can cause issues with the threading, fixings or plastic. If your supply hose is damaged or connections are damaged then it may be more suitable to replace.

If your faucet body is cracked or heavily corroded then it may need replacing.

Low Flow/Clogged Aerator

Often the best place to start with a weak or uneven flow of water is to check your aerator. This can cause issues with the flow as there can be build up of limescale or small particles.

Depending on your faucet design the aerator can be removed and cleaned. If this does not help then there could be an issue with a blocked cartridge, supply or other issue.

A professional can help identify what the issue is.

Should I Repair Or Replace My Faucet?

If your faucet is in good condition and there is an issue with a replaceable part then it may be a good idea to repair. If there is damage to the body, parts are not available or it keeps failing then it may be better to replace.

Reasons To Repair:

  • Leak from a replaceable part (cartridge, washer, seal).
  • Handle or aerator requires a compatible replacement part.
  • Body and connections are in a usable condition.
  • Cost of repairs are reasonable.

Reasons To Replace:

  • Cracked, heavily corroded or damaged body.
  • No suitable internal parts available.
  • Multiple parts have failed or it keeps failing.
  • You want a different style or extra features.

Before replacing check how many mounting holes there are, what type of sink you have, size of faucet and what type of supply you have. A new faucet may have different requirements for a spray or filter.

Ensure your new faucet will be compatible with your existing sink and water connections. If there are changes to the connections or mounting then discuss this with us. How To Avoid Issues

Keeping on top of your faucet can help avoid unnecessary leaks and issues.

  • Clean your aerators when they start to cause issues with the water flow due to limescale.
  • Don't force your faucets if they are stiff. Don't over tighten.
  • Check for any signs of dampness on visible connections and under your sink.
  • Use cleaning products that are suitable for your faucet finish. Follow the manufacturers guidelines.
  • Don't let your faucet drip continuously.
  • Keep any information on your model of faucet and any relevant installation documents.

If your faucet suddenly starts to leak then stop using it. If you can safely turn off the relevant shutoff valve then do so. If you cannot turn off the valve or it is still leaking then ask for a professional to help to minimise any potential water damage.
