When Will I Need a New Faucet?

A new faucet can help improve the usability of your sink. If your current faucet is damaged, old, difficult to use or not suitable for your planned renovations then installing a new one may be the right choice.

Old or Damaged Faucet

Older faucets can suffer from wear and tear on the inside. This can cause corrosion, loose handles and constant dripping. Whilst some of these issues can be repaired, if your faucet is damaged then it may be more practical to replace.

A new faucet can also update the look of your space. With modern faucets offering sleek handles, a range of finishes and features to make everyday tasks easier. When choosing your new faucet make sure it is durable, easy to clean and suitable for your current sink.

Renovating Kitchen/Bathroom

One of the most common reasons people install new faucets is due to renovating their kitchen or bathroom. If you are changing your sink, countertop, vanity or other items then it is worth checking if the new faucet will fit.

The number and spacing of your mounting holes can impact what type of faucet you can get. If you have a 3 hole sink you may not be able to get a single hole faucet unless you have the right plate.

Dripping, Loose Handle or Difficult to Operate

If your faucet is dripping, has a loose handle or difficult to turn then there could be wear and tear on the inside. However, your faucet doesn't always need to be replaced.

If your faucet is in good condition then a faulty cartridge, washer or other suitable component can be repaired. A professional can help you determine if it is best to repair or replace.

Types of Faucets

Choosing the right type of faucet for your home will depend on your sink, space available and plumbing connections.

Kitchen Faucet Installation

For Kitchen faucet installation huntington beach, kitchen faucets need to be suitable for everyday use. Common types of faucets available are standard spout, pull down sprayer, pull out and side sprayer.

When installing your kitchen faucet you should consider:

  • How high and long the spout on your faucet is.
  • How much space you have under your cabinets/shelves.
  • Will it fit with your current mounting holes.
  • How much space you have under your sink.
  • Do you need a sprayer?

A pull down faucet can help with rinsing your dishes and getting into the corners of your sink. However, your hose and sprayer will need enough space under your sink.

Bathroom Sink Faucet Installation

There are a few different types of faucets available for bathrooms. Single hole, centreset and widespread. This will depend on your sink/ vanity and how far apart your mounting holes are. If you need Bathroom faucet installation huntington beach, check your sink and mounting setup before choosing a model.

A compact single handle may be better for a small vanity. A widespread can give you a different look on a bigger countertop. When buying your new faucet make sure you know what size it is and how it will mount.

When installing a bathroom faucet you may need to check the condition of your water lines, mounting and drain. If you are replacing your sink/vanity make sure the faucet and components are suitable.

Single/Double Handle

Single handle faucets have one handle to control the water flow and temperature. These can be a great option if you have a small amount of space on your countertop.

Double handle faucets have separate hot and cold handles. Some homeowners prefer this traditional style to match their bathroom/kitchen.

Not all sinks will be suitable for either of these options. Make sure you choose an option that will fit with your mounting and offer the functionality you want.

Pull Down/Pull Out/Specialist

Specialist faucets can include touch activated, filtered water and other features. These can have specific requirements when installing.

An electric faucet may need a power source or battery compartment. Filtered water may need suitable tubing. Before choosing your model make sure you check the manufacturers installation instructions. If you need extra kit to fit under your sink make sure it will.

For Shower faucet installation huntington beach, the installation requirements can differ from those for a sink faucet. Check that the chosen shower faucet is compatible with the existing shower valve, plumbing connections and fixture setup before installation.

Turn Off Water

The first thing to do is turn off the hot and cold water. Once turned off you can detach your current faucet.

Older faucets can have corroded mounting, stuck nuts and difficult to reach connections. Be careful when removing to avoid any unnecessary damage to your sink, countertop and plumbing.

Prepare Sink/Countertop

Once your old faucet has been removed you should check the area where it was mounted. This should be free from any residue, corrosion or damage. There should be enough space for your new faucet and mounting.

Your installer should ensure that the base, washer and plate of your chosen faucet will fit with your sink. If your mounting holes are not suitable then you may need to get a different faucet or suitable adaptor.

Secure New Faucet

Once you have your new faucet you should follow the manufacturers instructions to secure it. Connect the water lines with suitable fittings.

Sometimes you will not need to change your drain however if you are also changing your sink you should check that it is suitable.

Test Faucet

Once installed you can slowly turn the water back on. Check that your faucet is working normally, hot and cold water is working and there are no leaks.

Your installer should check that the handle(s) are working smoothly and any pull down sprayer or extra features are working. If there is a leak then you should check the connections/ installation.

Things to Consider Before Buying

A little preparation can help you avoid getting a faucet that won't fit your sink.

  • How many mounting holes do you have? How far apart are they?
  • How high and long is the spout? How much space do you have?
  • Are your water lines suitable?
  • What type of material is your sink made from? Ensure your mounting is suitable.
  • Do you need a sprayer? Touch activated? Or specialist?
  • What finish would you like? Ensure it matches your room and how easy it is to clean.
  • Check the manufacturers instructions. What are the requirements? What comes in the box? Are there any limitations?

If you are replacing your existing faucet take pictures of your current faucet and plumbing underneath. Measuring and knowing what you want can help you find a suitable replacement.

Common Issues

Incorrect installation can cause leaks, instability, poor water flow and damage to your sink and countertop. There are a few common issues that can be avoided.

  • Wrong Faucet: If your faucet doesn't fit with your mounting holes or space then you may need extra components.
  • Over tightening: This can cause damage to your threading, fittings and mounting. Follow the manufacturers instructions.
  • Faulty Shut Off Valves: When replacing your faucet you may realise your current valve isn't closing properly or there is a leak. This should be dealt with before relying on it.
  • Incorrect Sealing: Ensure you are using the right gaskets, sealant etc for your faucet and sink. Using the wrong product can cause issues.
  • No Leak Check: Sometimes your connections can leak once the water is turned back on. Once installed check that your faucet is working and there are no leaks on any accessible connections. Once used check again.

If your plumbing under your sink is damaged or you cannot safely turn off your water then don't force your connections. A qualified plumber can check what the issue is and what the next step should be. Maintenance

Regular maintenance can help keep your faucet working properly. Small issues can be easily spotted.

  • Clean your faucet with suitable products.
  • Don't use abrasive materials.
  • Periodically check any accessible water lines for dampness.
  • Clean your aerator if you are having issues with water flow due to build up of minerals/debris.
  • If you are experiencing constant dripping, loose handles or odd behaviour then deal with this quickly.
  • Follow manufacturers instructions.

If you are having issues with low water pressure across your home then this may be an issue with your plumbing. Simply replacing your faucet may not help. If you are also having constant leaks then you should check your water lines, sink and countertop.

FAQ

How long does it take?

It will depend on the type of faucet, how easy it is to access and the condition of your current plumbing. If it is a simple replacement then it will take much less time than if you have corroded connections, unsuitable connections or changes to your sink. Your installer can give you a more useful time frame once they have seen the setup.

Can I replace my faucet without changing my sink?

Yes, you often can. As long as your new faucet is suitable for your sink (mounting, size and connections). Before purchasing check how many mounting holes you have and how far apart they are. If your new faucet isn't suitable then you may need a suitable adaptor or a different model.

Should I repair my faucet or replace?

If your faucet is in good condition and the issue is with a replaceable part inside then repairing may be suitable. If your current faucet is badly damaged, difficult to maintain or not suitable for your renovations then it may be more suitable to replace. This will depend on your faucet condition and if you can get suitable parts.

Do I need a plumber to install my faucet in Huntington Beach, CA?

You may be able to install a suitable faucet yourself following the manufacturers instructions. If you have corroded connections, faulty shut off valves, limited access or need any changes to your plumbing then it may be useful to get help from a professional. If you are unsure how to safely turn off your water then get help from a professional. If i need a plumber for installation, I should explain the type of faucet and any known plumbing issues before arranging the work. Check the credentials of a licensed plumber near me where licensing is applicable.

Can I install a pull down sprayer kitchen faucet?

Yes, as long as it is suitable for your sink and there is enough space under your sink. Check that the hose is suitable, there is enough space on your countertop and there is enough room for the hose to move. Some may require extra connections.

What should I check before buying a bathroom faucet?

How many mounting holes do you have? How far apart are they? How big is your sink? How long and high is the spout? Are your water lines suitable? Will the handle move without hitting a wall/mirror/etc?

Why is my new faucet leaking?

This can be due to loose/unsuitable connections, faulty gasket, incorrect installation or faulty existing water lines. If there is a large leak turn off your water and have the connections checked. Don't keep tightening your fittings.

Does it include replacing the drain?

Not always. They are separate jobs however sometimes can be done in one visit. When asking for your service make sure you know if it includes replacing your pop up drain assembly, drain connections or other sink components.
