Signs of Hidden Water Leaks

Sometimes there may not be a visible puddle from the water leak. Leaks may occur within pipes behind walls, under flooring or within your concrete slabs. Huntington Beach hidden leak detection can help investigate suspected leaks when the source is not immediately visible.

Unexpected Increase in Water Usage

If you're experiencing an unexpected increase in your water bill it may be worth getting a water leak investigation. This could be due to a water leak but could also be due to irrigation, running toilet or just a change in how much water is being used.

To investigate, you can turn off any faucets or appliances that use water. Check that your water meter is still showing water usage if you're able to safely get to it. Follow your water meter manufacturers guidance. Water meter results can be impacted by automatic irrigation systems.

Wet Patches, Staining and Flaking Paint

Water leaks can cause discolouration, bubbles in paint, warped flooring and wet cabinets. This could be caused by a water leak however it could also be due to a leak in your roof, condensation or outside water.

If the leak is near a bathroom you may want to investigate the plumbing in the area. If it's near an external wall you may wish to do a more general investigation.

Mouldy Smells and Mold

If you are experiencing mouldy smells it could be due to dampness in materials. If you are also experiencing mouldy discolouration, soft drywall and regular water then finding the source of the water is an important step.

Not all mouldy smells are due to a water leak in your plumbing, however a qualified professional can help to identify if it could be a plumbing issue. They can advise you on further investigations.

Running Water

If you're hearing running water in your pipes when all your fixtures are off then it could be a water leak. However it could also be caused by running toilets, water treatment equipment and irrigation.

You can check the accessible fixtures and appliances first. If you're still experiencing running water then having a water leak investigation can help identify the issue.

Common Water Leak Issues

The location of the leak will impact how it is investigated and repaired. Knowing the most common issues can help you explain the symptoms you're experiencing. Huntington Beach leak detection and Huntington Beach water leak detection may involve different investigation methods depending on whether the suspected leak is inside the property, beneath the foundation or underground.

Slab Leak

A slab leak can occur when there is an issue with the plumbing below your concrete foundation. Signs of a slab leak could include unexpected dampness, warm/wet flooring in certain areas and unusual high water usage.

This doesn't guarantee there is a slab leak however as the plumbing is behind the concrete it can be difficult to identify. Professionals can use water leak detection to help identify the likely source. For suspected leaks beneath a concrete foundation, plumbing leak detection Huntington Beach professionals may consider the pipe location, symptoms and condition of the property when choosing an investigation method.

Behind Walls and Flooring

Leaks can occur within your water supply pipes behind walls, under flooring or in your ceiling. Leaks can occur at connections, joins and damaged areas causing water to seep into surrounding materials.

Investigating the dampness and plumbing in the area can help identify the likely source. This can help prevent unnecessary damage when opening up walls or flooring.

Pipe, Fixtures and Water Supply Leak

Some leaks are easy to identify. Leaks can occur at faucets, toilet connections, damaged supply hoses and loose plumbing fittings.

Checking the connections can help identify if there is any dampness. If there is a visible leak then it may be relatively easy to repair. However if there is hidden dampness or regular dampness then it could be a more complex issue.

Underground Water Leak

Water supply leaks can occur underground in your yard, drive, pathways or landscaping. Signs of a water leak could include unexpected dampness, damp patches and changes in water pressure.

However dampness can occur outside due to irrigation, drainage and rainwater. Getting a suitable investigation can help identify if it could be a water supply issue. Hidden leak detection Huntington Beach investigations may be considered when the suspected leak is not visible at the surface.

Water Leak Detection Methods

Depending on where the pipes are, what type of leak you suspect and how your property is built professionals can use various methods. When comparing leak detection services Huntington Beach CA, ask which investigation methods may be appropriate for your property and suspected leak.

Acoustic Water Leak Detection

Acoustic equipment can be used to detect the sound of water leaking from pressurised pipes. A trained operative can use specialist listening equipment to identify the likely source.

This can help identify some hidden or underground pressurised water lines. However results can vary due to background noise, pipe type, size of the leak and surrounding area.

Thermal Imaging

Thermal imaging equipment can be used to detect changes in the surface temperatures. This can sometimes help identify the area that is affected by dampness or hot water leaks.

Thermal imaging cannot guarantee that there is a plumbing leak however temperature changes can be caused by other issues.

Moisture Meter & Visual Inspection

Moisture meters can be used to help identify how damp the area is. Visual inspections can help identify any staining, swelling, rusting or other signs.

This can help identify what areas have been affected however it may not tell you the exact source. Further investigations may be required to identify if it is an issue with your plumbing.

Pressure Testing

Depending on your plumbing a pressure test may help identify if your water line is maintaining pressure. The test needs to be suitable for the type of pipe.

A professional may use a combination of pressure testing, acoustic equipment, visual inspections or others to help identify the likely issue. This will ensure they have enough evidence.

What to Do if you Suspect a Water Leak

Simple steps can be taken to prevent further water damage.

  • Turn off the nearest suitable water valve if it is safe to do so.
  • If you cannot isolate the leak then consider turning off the main water supply to your property.
  • Move items away from any damp areas to protect them.
  • Don't touch any electrical equipment, switches or sockets in the damp area. If there is water near any electrical equipment keep clear and contact the correct qualified professional.
  • Take pictures of any visible damage to record it. This can help with any potential claims.
  • Try not to open any walls or flooring until the likely source has been identified.

If there is a large leak, the water is spreading quickly or there is a bulge in your ceiling then try to keep safe and contact a professional quickly. Don't enter any area that may be unsafe. FAQ

How do I know if I have a hidden water leak?

Symptoms can include unexpected increase in water usage, wet patches, flaking paint, warped flooring, mouldy smells and running water. These symptoms can have other causes so testing may be required to identify if there is an issue with your plumbing.

Can a plumber find a leak without damaging walls or flooring?

Often non-invasive methods can help to narrow down the likely source of the leak. Acoustic equipment, thermal imaging and moisture meters can be useful in the right circumstances. However some leaks will require minimal access.

What is slab leak detection?

Slab leak detection is when a suspected leak is investigated in a pipe under your concrete foundation. Depending on your plumbing and symptoms a professional can use acoustic equipment, pressure testing or other methods to narrow down the likely source.

How much does water leak detection cost in Huntington Beach, CA?

The cost will depend on where the leak is suspected, what type of building you have, what equipment is required and how complex the investigation. Ask for a price specific to your project. Ask if further investigations, documentation and repairs are included.

Can a water leak cause me to have a high water bill?

Yes, a leaking water supply line, running toilet or other plumbing issues can cause increased water usage. Issues with irrigation and changes in normal usage can also cause this. Checking your water meter and investigating other possible causes can help identify the source.

Is water leak detection the same as repairs?

No, the aim of water leak detection is to identify or narrow down the source of the leak. Repairs will focus on fixing the damaged part. Some providers will offer both however some may refer you to another professional.

What should I do if my pipe is leaking?

If it is safe turn off the correct water valve or main water supply. Keep clear of any electrical equipment that is wet and any unsafe areas. Try to protect your items.
